## Deviations from the plan

1. **Absolute wander heading** per pick, not a per-tick offset (Task 1): a per-tick offset circles
   ants at v/ω ≈ 8 u, which spec §5.4 already records. The fallback uses it too; `clearAhead`,
   `freeDistanceDir` and a cached ray fan keep the bench in budget.
2. **A collision test was replaced, not weakened** (Task 3): exact `antRadius` clearance every tick
   plus a gets-past assertion — stricter than the "stops at the wall" premise look-ahead breaks.
3. **The spatial hash is sparse** — counting sort over occupied cells, 0.005 ms against 0.151 ms
   dense; stuck work is gated on `timeSinceLastMarker > stuckTimeout`, when the degrade flag can
   first be set. **`maxCells`** bounds BFS-expanded solid cells, not a radius (Tasks 4–5).
4. **`emissionDecay` stays 0.11** (Task 7): the plan's 0.02–0.04 inverts the ant's own trail at
   read time (spec §5.4 needs decay > ln 2 / half-life) and measured 0 deliveries at every
   distance. Its 3× D=1000 minute-3 target (193 → 579) is unreachable at any decay it allows.
5. **Two think fixtures changed, not their assertions** (Task 8): a homing ant curves, so
   straight-line distance lags its path until it turns. **Two territory tests were rewritten**
   (Task 9): their one-owner-cell rule was replaced by design; shared-cell, per-layer degrade and
   owner-arbitration cover it.
6. **`steerAroundWalls` was rewritten** (Task 9b, `55bce5e`): one side from the uid, searching from
   the current heading, instead of a side redrawn every 0.25–0.6 s and a search from the target,
   which left blocked ants oscillating. Paths change everywhere; the maze dies without it.
7. **Maze `energyBurn` is 0.2, not 0.3** (Task 9b): at 0.3 the search budget is 133 s and the route
   takes 157 s. **"Minutes 12–14"** is read as [12:00, 15:00). The plan's `task-start`/`task-done`
   scripts are absent from this plugin build; the ledger was kept by hand.
8. **The trail-fade test the plan committed in its review focus was added at review**, failing
   first: at the shipped 20 s half-life a full-strength deposit is 0.0055 after 150 s and crosses
   `MARKER_DETECT_MIN` at 199 s. `MARKER_HALF_LIFE` is now **15 s** (0.00098 at 150 s); reach, maze,
   boulder and bench were re-measured afterwards.
9. **"Reset parameters" now resets to the scenario's own defaults** (`paramsForSetup`), not
   `defaultParams()`: one click used to strip the maze's burn override and leave it unwinnable.
