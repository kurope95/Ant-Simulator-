## Deviations from the plan
1. Cluster test uses a 40 u **radius** (80 u across), not a 40 u diameter. 200 ants at 4.5 u spacing need a radius of about 33 u, so the 20 u version cannot pass.
2. Cluster test turns avoidance off (`avoidMaxSpeedFactor` = 0) because it tests the PBD pass. With avoidance on it ends at 4.05 u apart.
3. Coincident-ants test runs 60 full ticks, not 60 bare pair passes.
4. With collisions off, the Collisions phase is the pre-M4 pass (one clamp, wall resolve, clamp). The plan's loop runs 2 iterations, which changes the checksum.
5. Avoidance arrays go into the checksum only while collisions are on, so the collisions-off checksum stays equal to pre-M4.
6. Avoidance rule: the lateral part of the relative miss, not the tangent projection in spec §6.1. Recorded in the spec.
7. "Infinite food" uses the existing `infinite` source flag. A large finite amount made the food radius cover the map.
8. `--scenario crowd` is a new bench-only value. The old `--scenario sandbox` alias still maps to a preset. `highway` appears in the builder menu, as the plan wanted.
9. The order-independence test covers the two passes only (`computeAvoidance`, `resolveAntOverlaps`), not the Think phase.

## Open questions for Marek
1. Lane rule. The away-from-the-other-ant sidestep is mirror-symmetric, so off-axis pairs repel instead of forming lanes. Options: always sidestep to the own right (tested: 0.56 lanes, overlap worse), or a tie-break that keeps one side for a pair. Needs a decision.
2. Overlap is 3.6% with PBD at 2 iterations. Is 3 iterations (CPU cost) acceptable, or should target stay at 1%?
3. Throughput at 1500 u is 61% of M2 (51 vs 63 needed). Is the food-queue exemption from the review focus worth trying?
4. Performance: 2000 ants at 8.6 ms is above the 6 ms target. Cost is mostly the per-ant avoidance and PBD queries in JS; fast paths are the next step.
