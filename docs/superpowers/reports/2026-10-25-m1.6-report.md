## Terrain shader cost (the 3 ms bar)
`captures/terrain-cost-copy.html` (git-ignored) runs the real shader from `dist/` on a full 2880 × 1620 quad, 60 passes, synced with `readPixels`. Adreno X1-45 via ANGLE D3D11, built-in browser, plugged in, MSAA on.
- Full pass: 3.9 ms on a mixed map; 2.4 ms on open ground; 7.0 ms with rock in every cell.
- Copy of the cached terrain to the screen: 0.74–0.85 ms.
- Plan fallback applied: the terrain is drawn into a screen-size copy and redrawn only when the camera, viewport, mask, theme or the 10 Hz water shimmer changes.
- **Idle frames cost about 0.8 ms. Camera-move and shimmer frames cost 3.9 ms on a mixed map, over 3 ms. Open.**
- Shader changes from the plan's text (all in the ledger): ordered detail-smoothstep edges; quintic fade; four-tap mask average for shores; exact open-ground early-out; explicit LOD.
